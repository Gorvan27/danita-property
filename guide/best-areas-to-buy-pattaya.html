Best areas to buy a condo in Pattaya

Jomtien

A long, popular beach south of the city. Best value per square metre near the sea and a big expat community.

Pratumnak

The quiet, upscale hill between Jomtien and Pattaya — residential, green, close to both.

Wongamat & Naklua

North Pattaya's most upscale beachfront, with premium sea-view towers and a calmer atmosphere.

Central & South Pattaya

City living on Beach Road and Second Road — walkable, close to nightlife, shopping and transport.

Na Jomtien

Newer, quieter developments further south, popular for modern beachfront projects and long-term living.

FAQ

Which Pattaya area is best for beachfront?
Wongamat and Naklua in the north offer the most upscale beachfront; Jomtien has a long, popular beach with better value.
Where is the quietest place to buy in Pattaya?
Pratumnak and Na Jomtien are the calmest, most residential areas.
Where is the best value in Pattaya?
Jomtien generally offers the best price per square metre close to the beach.
